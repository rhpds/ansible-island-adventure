# Module 01 — Welcome to the island

### Brief Overview

This module introduces the Ansible Island Adventure concept and its pirate-themed narrative. Attendees learn what the event looks like from a customer's perspective, how challenges are structured across multiple difficulty tiers, and why gamification drives engagement. The module sets the stage for the rest of the lab by showing how the event accommodates mixed skill levels, from first-time Ansible users to experienced practitioners.

### Audience and Time

- **Personas:** Solution Architects, TAMs, partner engineers
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Basic Ansible concepts (playbooks, roles, inventories); some exposure to OpenShift
- **Estimated duration:** 15 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Explore the Ansible Island Adventure platform from the customer participant perspective, including the pirate-themed narrative, challenge flow, and scoring mechanics
- Analyze how challenge categories and difficulty tiers — warm-up tasks, CLI exploration, Ansible Automation Platform controller orchestration, code creation, trivia, and the Ansible Quest video game — accommodate audiences of varying skill levels
- Demonstrate the value proposition of gamified enablement events for driving customer and partner engagement

### Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Introduction to Ansible Island Adventure | 5 min |
| 2 | The customer experience walkthrough | 5 min |
| 3 | Challenge categories and difficulty tiers | 5 min |

### Detailed Steps

1. Read the introduction to the Ansible Island Adventure concept and its pirate-themed narrative
2. Review the event flow from the customer's perspective: registration, receiving handouts, accessing the CTFd scoring platform, and working through challenges
3. Examine how challenges are structured into categories: Warm Up, Let's get started, At the Beach, Treasure Hunt, Side Quest, and Trivia
4. Review the difficulty tiers and how they allow mixed-skill audiences to participate meaningfully
5. Observe how the scoring system and leaderboard drive engagement and friendly competition
6. Understand why gamification is effective for technical enablement and how it differentiates this event from a standard workshop

### Key Takeaways

- Ansible Island Adventure is a proven, customer-facing CTF event built around Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform
- The pirate-themed narrative and gamified challenge structure drive higher engagement than traditional workshops
- Multiple difficulty tiers ensure participants at all skill levels — from beginners to experts — stay engaged
- The event is already built and proven with customers; this lab teaches how to deploy and deliver it independently

### Infrastructure Notes

No infrastructure is required for this module. This is a presentation and walkthrough module.

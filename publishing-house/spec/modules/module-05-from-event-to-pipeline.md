# Module 05 — From event to pipeline

### Brief Overview

This module shifts focus from the technical delivery to the business impact of Ansible Island Adventure events. Attendees learn how to position the event for customers and partners, capture participant data for lead generation, measure engagement through CTFd metrics, and build a follow-up plan to convert event attendees into qualified pipeline. This is what transforms a fun technical event into a measurable business outcome.

### Audience and Time

- **Personas:** Solution Architects, TAMs, partner engineers
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Completion of Module 4 (firsthand experience with the event); understanding of the CTFd platform and its data capture capabilities
- **Estimated duration:** 20 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Demonstrate how to position Ansible Island Adventure to customers and partners as both an enablement experience and a marketing engagement tool
- Analyze participant engagement metrics from CTFd — challenge completion rates, time on platform, hint usage — to identify attendee interest areas and skill levels
- Create a targeted follow-up plan to convert event participants into qualified leads based on demonstrated interest areas and engagement patterns

### Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1 | Positioning the event | 5 min |
| 2 | Lead generation and data capture | 5 min |
| 3 | Engagement metrics and reporting | 5 min |
| 4 | Conversion follow-up planning | 5 min |

### Detailed Steps

1. Review positioning guidance: how to pitch Ansible Island Adventure to customers and partners as a combined enablement and marketing experience
2. Examine how participant contact information is captured through the CTFd platform during registration
3. Navigate the CTFd admin panel to review participant data collected during Module 4
4. Analyze engagement metrics from the session: review challenge completion rates across categories, identify which challenges saw the most activity, and note time-on-platform data
5. Examine hint usage patterns to understand where participants struggled and what topics need follow-up enablement
6. Discuss how engagement metrics map to participant interest areas: heavy controller usage suggests orchestration interest, strong CLI completion suggests automation maturity
7. Review the follow-up framework: how to craft targeted outreach based on demonstrated interest areas
8. Build a sample follow-up plan using the engagement data from the Module 4 session, identifying which attendees are strong leads and what their likely next steps would be
9. Discuss how to report event success metrics to stakeholders and justify future events

### Key Takeaways

- Ansible Island Adventure is not just a technical event — it is a pipeline generation tool when paired with proper follow-up
- CTFd captures participant data and engagement metrics that reveal interest areas and skill levels
- Challenge completion patterns, hint usage, and time on platform provide actionable insight for targeted outreach
- A structured follow-up plan turns event participation into qualified pipeline

### Infrastructure Notes

- CTFd admin access is required to review participant metrics and engagement data
- Engagement data from Module 4 is used as a live example for analysis exercises in this module
- No additional infrastructure beyond what was deployed in Module 3 is needed

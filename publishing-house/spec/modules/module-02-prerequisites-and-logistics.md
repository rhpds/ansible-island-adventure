# Module 02 — Prerequisites and logistics

### Brief Overview

This module covers the practical requirements for running an Ansible Island Adventure event. Attendees learn about infrastructure needs, staffing recommendations, pre-event preparation steps, room setup, and timing considerations. By the end of this module, attendees will understand everything they need to plan and prepare for a customer-facing event.

### Audience and Time

- **Personas:** Solution Architects, TAMs, partner engineers
- **Prerequisites for this module:** Completion of Module 1; familiarity with the Red Hat Demo Platform
- **Estimated duration:** 10 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Verify the infrastructure requirements for running Ansible Island Adventure, including ordering the pre-deployed environment from the Red Hat Demo Platform
- Manage pre-event preparation tasks: ordering environments, completing organizer post-deploy and readiness checks, distributing service-link handouts and credentials, and setting up shortened URLs
- Configure event logistics including staffing ratios, facilitator roles, room setup, and timing for groups of varying size

### Lab Structure

### Detailed Steps

1. Review the required infrastructure: a pre-deployed Ansible Island Adventure environment ordered from the Red Hat Demo Platform
2. Note the recommended instructor-to-participant ratios and facilitator roles for different group sizes
3. Walk through the preparation checklist: order with sufficient lead time, complete organizer post-deploy, verify boards and access, prepare the unlock key, and distribute service-link handouts with passwords supplied separately
4. Review room setup considerations: desk formation for collaborative work, laptop requirements for participants, and network connectivity needs
5. Review the customer event's 1–1.5-hour agenda: 10 minutes welcome, 10 minutes access/workspace setup, 30–45 minutes scored challenges including Warm Up, and 10–25 minutes wrap-up. Scoring never exceeds 45 minutes; optional extended networking/debrief follows the scheduled event
6. Distinguish the two-hour training agenda: Module 3 has 10 minutes setup, 35 minutes scored play including Warm Up, and 5 minutes debrief, followed by instructor administration in Module 4

### Key Takeaways

- A pre-deployed Ansible Island Adventure environment ordered from the Red Hat Demo Platform is the core infrastructure requirement
- Pre-event preparation — environment ordering, credential distribution, handout creation — must be completed before the event day
- Staffing ratios and facilitator roles are critical for a smooth event experience
- Customer events last 1–1.5 hours with one 30–45-minute scoring window including Warm Up; optional extended networking/debrief is additional
- Organizer provisioning and post-deploy occur before the event and outside the training clock

### Infrastructure Notes

- Environments are ordered through the Red Hat Demo Platform and arrive with AIA pre-deployed
- Lead time is required for environment provisioning — plan accordingly
- Network access at the event venue must support participant connections to the OpenShift cluster

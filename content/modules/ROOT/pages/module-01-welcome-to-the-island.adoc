== Introduction to Ansible Island Adventure

Ansible Island Adventure is a gamified *Capture the Flag (CTF)* competition built around Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform.
Wrapped in a pirate-themed narrative, participants solve challenges that exercise real Ansible skills — from basic CLI commands to orchestrating workloads through the Automation Controller.

The event is already built and *proven with customers*.
This lab teaches you how to use it, deliver it, and customize it for your own engagements.

== The Customer Experience

When a customer attends an Ansible Island Adventure event, the experience follows this flow:

. *Registration* — Participants receive a handout with their login credentials, shortened URLs, and a brief overview of the event.
. *Access CTFd* — They log into the CTFd scoring platform using the credentials on their handout.
. *Work through challenges* — Challenges are organized into categories of increasing difficulty. Each completed challenge yields a _flag_ that participants submit to CTFd for points.
. *Leaderboard competition* — A live leaderboard drives friendly competition and keeps energy high throughout the event.
. *Debrief* — Facilitators recap the challenges and connect the experience to Ansible Automation Platform capabilities.

The entire customer-facing event typically runs *2–3 hours*, depending on audience size and skill level.

== Challenge Categories and Difficulty Tiers

Challenges are organized into six categories that progressively build Ansible skills:

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
| Category | Description | Skill Level

| *Warm Up*
| Account setup, find Ansible documentation and collections
| Beginner

| *Let's Get Started*
| Discover Ansible core and AAP versions in the environment
| Beginner

| *At the Beach*
| Clone repos from Gitea, run playbooks from CLI, decrypt Vault secrets
| Intermediate

| *Treasure Hunt*
| Create projects in AAP Controller, configure credentials, build job templates, deploy a web app
| Intermediate–Advanced

| *Side Quest*
| Play the Ansible Quest interactive video game
| Fun / Exploration

| *Trivia*
| Test knowledge of Ansible history and ecosystem
| All levels
|===

This progression ensures participants at *every skill level* stay engaged:

* Beginners can complete Warm Up and Let's Get Started challenges and feel accomplishment.
* Experienced practitioners push through Treasure Hunt and the advanced verifier-based challenges.
* Everyone enjoys Side Quest and Trivia as a change of pace.

== Why Gamification Works

Traditional workshops follow a linear, instructor-led format. Gamification changes the dynamic:

* *Self-paced* — Participants move at their own speed. Fast learners aren't bored; beginners aren't left behind.
* *Intrinsic motivation* — Points, hints, and the leaderboard create engagement without requiring constant instructor direction.
* *Hands-on by default* — Every point requires doing something real on the platform. There is no passive observation.
* *Memorable* — The pirate theme and competitive element make the event memorable. Customers talk about it afterward.

When paired with follow-up (covered in Module 6), an Ansible Island Adventure event becomes a *pipeline generation tool* — not just a workshop.

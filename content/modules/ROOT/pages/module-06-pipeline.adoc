== Positioning and Lead Generation

Ansible Island Adventure is not just a technical event — it is a *pipeline generation tool* when paired with proper positioning and follow-up.

=== How to Position the Event

When pitching Ansible Island Adventure to customers and partners, emphasize:

* *Hands-on experience* — Participants walk away having actually used Ansible Automation Platform, not just watched a demo.
* *Low barrier to entry* — The gamified format is approachable for mixed-skill audiences. Beginners feel welcome; experts feel challenged.
* *Fun and memorable* — The pirate theme and competitive element make it stand out from standard vendor workshops. Customers talk about it after the event.
* *Customizable* — Challenges can be tailored to the customer's industry, use case, or technical maturity (as you learned in Module 5).

=== Effective Positioning Scenarios

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Scenario | Positioning

| *Customer enablement day*

== Engagement Metrics and Analysis

CTFd captures rich participant data that reveals interest areas and skill levels. Let's analyze the data from your session.

=== Access the CTFd Admin Panel

. Open your CTFd instance: {ctfd_url}
. Log in as admin (the facilitator account).
. Navigate to the *Admin Panel* (gear icon or `/admin` URL path).

=== Key Metrics to Review

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Metric | What It Tells You

| *Challenge completion rates*
| Which challenges were most/least completed? Low completion on a specific challenge may indicate it's too hard for the audience — or that the topic needs more enablement.

| *Time on platform*
| How long did participants stay engaged? Higher time-on-platform correlates with deeper learning and higher lead quality.

| *Hint usage*
| Which challenges required the most hints? High hint usage identifies topics where participants need follow-up training or enablement resources.

| *Category progression*
| How far did participants get through the challenge categories? Participants who reached Treasure Hunt demonstrated real AAP proficiency.

| *Leaderboard distribution*
| Is there a wide score spread or a tight cluster? Wide spreads indicate mixed skill levels; tight clusters indicate a well-matched audience.
|===

=== Map Engagement to Interest Areas

Challenge completion patterns reveal participant interest areas:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Challenge Pattern | Likely Interest Area

| Strong Warm Up + At the Beach completion
| Ansible fundamentals — good candidate for Ansible Getting Started training

| Strong Treasure Hunt completion (projects, templates, credentials)
| AAP Controller orchestration — interested in scaling automation

| Completed "You found a treasure chest" (web server deployment)
| Infrastructure automation — interested in end-to-end automation workflows

| Active Side Quest participation
| Ansible developer tools ecosystem — interested in testing, linting, CI/CD

| High Trivia engagement
| Ansible community and ecosystem — potential community champion
|===

== Follow-Up Planning and Resources

=== Build a Follow-Up Plan

Using the engagement data from CTFd, build a targeted follow-up plan:

. *Identify strong leads* — Participants who completed Treasure Hunt challenges and stayed on-platform for the full event duration are your highest-quality leads.
. *Segment by interest* — Group participants by their challenge completion patterns (see table above) to tailor follow-up messaging.
. *Schedule follow-up within 1 week* — Momentum fades quickly. Send a personalized follow-up email referencing their specific achievements in the event.
. *Provide next steps* — Include links to relevant resources:
** https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/management/ansible[Ansible Automation Platform product page]
** https://www.redhat.com/en/services/training/do007-ansible-essentials-simplicity-automation-technical-overview[Ansible Essentials training]
** https://catalog.demo.redhat.com[Red Hat Demo Platform] — for continued hands-on access
** Offer a deeper technical workshop or proof-of-concept engagement

= Ansible Island Adventure: Facilitator Training
:navtitle: Lab Overview

== Welcome, Future Facilitator!

Ansible Island Adventure is a proven, customer-facing Capture the Flag (CTF) competition built around *Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform*.
This "teach the teacher" lab enables you — a Solution Architect, TAM, or partner engineer — to independently order, deliver, customize, and generate pipeline from Ansible Island Adventure events.

Your environment is *already deployed* from the Red Hat Demo Platform (RHDP).
You will play through the challenges as a participant would, explore the running architecture and instructor toolkit, modify challenge content, and learn how to position the event for pipeline generation.

== What You Will Learn

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

. Navigate the Ansible Island Adventure environment on Red Hat OpenShift, identifying the architecture components (CTFd, Gitea, MariaDB, Redis, AAP, Dev Spaces) and understanding the deployment artifacts that provisioned them.
. Demonstrate how to facilitate a customer-facing CTF event using the provided facilitation guidance, handout templates, and pacing recommendations.
. Configure challenge definitions, difficulty tiers, and content to tailor the event for specific customer use cases or partner engagements.
. Analyze participant engagement metrics and create a follow-up plan to convert attendees into qualified leads.

== Lab Duration

This lab takes approximately *2 hours* to complete:

This is the facilitator-training duration. A customer-facing event is shorter: *1–1.5 hours*, including *30–45 minutes of challenges with a strict 45-minute limit*, followed by optional additional networking and debrief.

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| Module | Title | Duration

| 1 | Welcome to the Island | 10 min
| 2 | Prerequisites and Logistics | 10 min
| 3 | Play the Game | 50 min
| 4 | Explore the Environment | 20 min
| 5 | Customize and Extend | 20 min
| 6 | From Event to Pipeline | 10 min
|===

Module 3 includes 10 minutes of participant access and workspace setup, 35 minutes of scored play including Warm Up, and 5 minutes of debrief and saving results. Module 4 then introduces the instructor workspace, vault setup, and a controlled reset of your personal training board. Keep your gameplay notes for Module 6; after the reset, one new Warm Up solve will demonstrate progress preservation in Module 5.

Catalog provisioning, organizer post-deploy configuration, and readiness checks happen *before* the two-hour training session. Customer-event timing is described separately in Module 2.

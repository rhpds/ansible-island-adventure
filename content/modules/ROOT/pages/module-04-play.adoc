== Warm Up Challenges

These challenges get everyone started and verify basic access.

=== Write Your Name in the Sand

. In CTFd, click on your username in the top-right corner, then *Settings*.
. Change your *User Name* to your real name (e.g., Jane Smith).
. Change your *Email* to your real email address.
. Return to the challenges page, open "Write your name in the sand," and submit the flag: `DONE`

=== Unlock the Island

The facilitator provides a key to unlock the remaining challenges.

[NOTE]
====
In a customer event, *you* are the facilitator. You control when to reveal this key to pace the event.
The unlock key is defined in `vars/flags.yml` (vault-encrypted).
====

=== Ansible Docs and Collections

. *Ansible Docs* — Find the URL to the official Ansible documentation and submit it.
. *Ansible Collections* — Find the community collection hub URL and submit it.
. *Ansible Certified Collections* — Find the URL for Red Hat's certified content hub.

====

== Let's Get Started Challenges

These challenges verify that participants can access their environment tools.

=== The Navigator's Run

. Open your *Dev Spaces* workspace: {devspaces_url}
. Open a terminal within the workspace.
. Run the following command to find the Ansible core version:
+
[source,bash]
----
ansible --version
----
+
. Submit the version number as your flag in CTFd.

=== The High Tower Signal Strength

. Open the *AAP Console*: {aap_url}
. Log in with your credentials: `{user}` / `{password}`
. Find the *Platform Version* on the dashboard or under the help menu (*?* icon → *About*).
. Submit the platform version as your flag.

=== AAP Controller Version

. Still in the AAP Console, look for the *Controller version* — this is different from the Platform version.
. Check the *About* dialog or the footer of the Automation Controller UI.
. Submit the controller version as your flag.

== At the Beach Challenges

These challenges introduce Ansible CLI skills.

=== Great Git-Away

. Open *Gitea*: {gitea_url}
. Log in with your credentials.
. Find the repository that was created for you and note its name.
. Submit the repository name as your flag in CTFd.

=== Hello World Beach

. In your Dev Spaces terminal, clone your repository from Gitea:
+
[source,bash,subs="attributes"]
----
cd ~
git clone https://{user}:{password}@$(echo {gitea_url} | sed 's|https://||')/{user}/ansible-challenge.git
cd ansible-challenge
----
+
. Run the `hello-world.yml` playbook:
+
[source,bash]
----
ansible-playbook hello-world.yml
----
+
. Watch the output for the keyword and submit it as your flag.

=== Message in a Bottle at the Beach

. In the `ansible-challenge` repository, find the file `message-in-a-bottle.txt`.
. Decrypt it using Ansible Vault with the passphrase `redhat`:
+
[source,bash]
----
ansible-vault view message-in-a-bottle.txt --ask-vault-pass
----
+
. Enter the passphrase `redhat` when prompted.
. Submit the decrypted message as your flag.

== Treasure Hunt Challenges

These challenges use the *Ansible Automation Platform Controller* to orchestrate automation.

=== Hello World Treasure Hunt

. In the AAP Controller ({aap_url}), go to *Automation Execution → Projects*.
. Click *Create project* and configure it:
* *Name:* `ansible-challenge`
* *Source Control Type:* Git
* *Source Control URL:* Your Gitea repository URL
* *Source Control Branch:* `treasurehunt`
. Save and wait for the project to sync successfully.
. Go to *Automation Execution → Templates* and click *Create template → Create job template*:
* *Name:* `Hello World`
* *Project:* `ansible-challenge`
* *Playbook:* `hello-world.yml`
* *Inventory:* Select an existing inventory (or create one with `localhost`)
. Launch the job and find the keyword in the output.
. Submit the keyword as your flag.

=== Secret Message Treasure Hunt

. Go to *Automation Execution → Infrastructure → Credentials*.
. Click *Create credential*:
* *Name:* `Vault Credential`
* *Credential Type:* Vault
* *Vault Password:* `AAP`
. Create a new *Job Template*:
* *Name:* `Secret Message`
* *Project:* `ansible-challenge`
* *Playbook:* `secret-word.yml`
* *Credentials:* Add both the *Vault Credential* you just created and any required machine credential
. Launch the job and find the secret message in the output.
. Submit the secret message as your flag.

=== You Found a Treasure Chest

. Create a new *Job Template*:
* *Name:* `Deploy Web Server`
* *Project:* `ansible-challenge`
* *Playbook:* `apache/apache_role_install.yml`
* *Inventory:* Your instructor inventory
* *Credentials:* Add both the *Machine Credential* and the *Vault Credential*
* *Enable Privilege Escalation:* Check this option
. Launch the job and wait for it to complete.
. Use `curl` from your Dev Spaces terminal to access the web page on your managed node:
+
[source,bash,subs="attributes"]
----
curl http://{managed_node}
----
+
. Find the treasure keyword in the response and submit it as your flag.

== Side Quest and Trivia

=== Ansible Quest Video Game

The https://l3acon.github.io/ansible-quest/[Ansible Quest] is an interactive video game that teaches Ansible developer tool concepts through exploration.

. Open the Side Quest challenges in CTFd.
. Launch the Ansible Quest game from the challenge description link.
. Explore the game world and find treasure chests containing flags for tools like `ansible-creator`, `ansible-lint`, `ansible-navigator`, `molecule`, and more.
. Submit each flag you discover.

== Debrief and Discussion

After completing the challenges, reflect on the experience from a *facilitator's perspective*:

* *Which challenges resonated most?* — At the Beach and Treasure Hunt provide the deepest hands-on learning.
* *Where did participants struggle?* — Common blockers include Gitea clone URLs, vault password confusion, and credential configuration in AAP.
* *How does audience composition affect pacing?* — Technical audiences blaze through Warm Up; non-technical audiences need more roamer support during At the Beach.
* *What would you customize for your customers?* — Module 5 covers how to modify challenge content.

== Key Takeaways

* Playing through the event as a participant is essential for effective facilitation — you must know the challenges firsthand.
* The challenge progression moves from basic Ansible awareness through CLI proficiency to controller orchestration, covering a wide skill spectrum.
* The scoring and hint system balances challenge difficulty with accessibility, keeping all skill levels engaged.
* Understanding which challenges resonate with different audiences helps you customize and pace future customer events.
